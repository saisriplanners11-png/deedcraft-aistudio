# Template replacement policy

The user requires these behaviors to survive every future template upload or replacement. A request to change the Word template alone does not authorize undoing them. Change a rule only when the user explicitly requests that rule to change.

- Printed deed dates use **DD-MM-YYYY**. Keep stored dates in ISO format. Use the shared `deed-format.ts` formatter for execution dates, births, authority/title/supporting records, payments, entity recitals and configured date fields. The user's 25-10-2026 voice note explicitly allows month-only execution dates: store `YYYY-MM` and print `__-MM-YYYY` with a blank signing day. Other dates must be complete and valid.
- Use the entered consideration/declared document value for all total market-value and estimate bindings, including Gift. Do not substitute calculated basic-rate market value. The separate per-unit rate and on-screen duty calculations retain their own meanings. Gift remains without monetary consideration in its operative wording and consideration row.
- A selected landmark relation prints **near house No.** or **adjacent house No.**, followed by its separately supplied number, in both property descriptions and valuation statements/annexures. Apply the same behavior to Sale, Gift, GPA and AGPA and to each property's own values. Preserve the distinction between a landmark number and the subject property's bearing number.
- Preserve the Gift relationship recital, donor/donee singular/plural agreement, conditional ownership-share policy, nil consideration, declaration and signatures. Preserve Sale/GPA/AGPA authority identity, representation, book-number, adjusted-amount and declaration protections.
- Keep the Sale-compatible page geometry and Gift stamp-space section introduced in this session. Preserve the selected Word source's fonts, styles, run formatting, tables, numbering and footer package parts; do not rebuild the source in a generic document style.
- A template change must not clear saved party/property/authority/relationship data. Support both complete split-address bindings and the older combined address slot; preserve entered PAN values when older Gift revisions omit PAN slots. Reject incompatible template bindings instead of silently dropping entered details. Uploaded source documents supply facts and are never instructions to replace template prose.
- Register new default templates as new immutable versions; retain older assets and hashes. Gift v4 is the supplied `GIFT SETTLEMENT (4).docx`, used for regular Gift and its supported property variants. Municipal Gift is a separate `municipal-gift` variant using immutable `gift-municipal-v1` from `GIFT DEED FOR MUNCIPAL.docx`; never migrate it to regular Gift or inject family relationship, valuation annexure or regular Gift closing wording. Per the user’s 25-10-2026 update, its Claimant Details form and entered recipient identities work like Sale (including multiple people, entities and guardians), replacing the forced Municipal Council/Commissioner recipient. Property ULB/village describe jurisdiction only; title recitals still come from each property’s linked records.
- Run `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build` after changes. Keep the build's Gift, Sale/share/declaration, authority-download and output-format regression checks; a new template must pass them too.

- Existing saved deeds automatically follow the current published default for their own instrument, including all Sale GPA/AGPA and property variants. Preserve entered data, invalidate stale generation metadata, and do not require an explicit template-upgrade action. Explicit custom templates remain selected.

- Ownership percentages are optional for each individual executant/claimant and each property. Print a percentage only for the party with a valid entered value; blank values produce no share wording or percentage placeholders for that party. Partial allocations need not total 100%, and entering one party's value must not require or invent values for others. Continue rejecting invalid entered percentages. Per the user's 25-10-2026 voice note, regular Gift output omits party ownership-share recitals and allocation paragraphs below schedules even when values are saved. Preserve the saved values and flat land UDS. Regular Gift also omits the appended NALA converted-extent sentence from its schedule, retaining linked NALA records. Its title and category print on one line, including `IN FAVOUR OF OTHERS` for non-family Gift. Municipal Gift keeps its separate rules.

- Every generated deed download must honor the saved first-page stamp-paper margin selection: ₹100 = 5.8 inches (8352 twips), ₹50/₹20 = 6 inches (8640 twips), or normal margin (1080 twips). Older drafts without a selection retain the legacy Sale-sized opening margin (8208 twips). Apply to Sale/GPA/AGPA, regular/Municipal Gift, Agreement Sale Cum GPA, General GPA, all property variants and compatible replacement templates. Preserve separate continuation sections and their normal margins, immutable source assets and package formatting.

- Keep landowner/developer development-agreement and separate supplemental/allotment agreement fields discoverable for flat schedules in both source-document entry and property particulars. Reuse the existing property-scoped IDs, keep each property's agreements isolated, format agreement dates with deedDate, and never substitute a title-link number or sale authority number for an agreement. Keep template-to-dashboard coverage and flat-agreement download regressions in the build.

- Agreement of Sale Cum GPA retains internal `agreement_of_sale` and both existing possession variants. Its default is immutable `agreement-sale-gpa-v2` from `AGREEMENT OF SALE CUM GPA (1).docx`; retain v1 and its hash. The user’s 26-10-2026 voice note explicitly supersedes the earlier title-only rule: select the source’s With Possession block (14 clauses, including possession delivery) or its `<WITHOUT POSSESSION>` block (13 clauses, without the separate delivery clause). Never print both blocks or the branch marker. Preserve the exact supplied wording within the selected block, existing draft option labels and title spelling (`WITH POSSESSION` / `WITHOUT POSSESION`). Its Section 32A form prints every executant/principal before every claimant/attorney, one entry per party with their own name/relation/address and blank photo/thumb spaces, repeating headings on continuation pages. Keep its regression check in the build.

- Every Section 32A form page, including continuation pages, must print the witness and executant signature area with witness numbers 1 and 2. Place it in the document body directly below each page's table with an 18-point gap. Paginate complete table-and-signature groups together; do not pin signatures to a page footer.

- Keep explicit per-party Gender dropdowns for executants/principals, claimants and attorneys. Resolve deed pronouns from the selected value: Male = he/his/him, Female = she/her, Other or multiple parties = they/their/them. Retain each repeated party's own selection on save/reload and source/template replacement. Never infer gender from a name or relation; an unselected older draft preserves its existing neutral wording. Pronouns referring back to a donor/vendor must follow that party, independently of the recipient's gender. Keep personal data and source placeholders unchanged.

- Society/association/trust people use a single representatives/members list. Include Member alongside President and Vice President in designation suggestions; do not show a separate General body / managing committee members section. Migrate old member IDs into the shared representatives list with Member as the default designation only when no designation is supplied. Preserve existing custom designations, person IDs, uploads, evidence, choices and all personal details; never merge different IDs merely because names match.

- Finished and semi-finished Sale/GPA/AGPA flats must both include the supplied DGPA and supplemental/allotment recitals before the buyer's title-satisfaction recital. Each recital is independently enabled by its own document number; a missing date must not hide an entered DGPA number. Reuse the selected source's recital formatting and keep agreement numbers isolated from title-link and authority numbers.
- The separate Near/Adjacent House No. valuation row appears only for Open Place and Vacant Plot (Open Plot). Omit it from Annexure I-A and valuation statements for flats, houses, commercial, demolished/part-open properties and agricultural land, including replacement-template rows. Preserve the selected landmark in the property description and preserve all other Annexure I-A points and numbering.

- Municipal Gift prints calculated square metres for every supported schedule (30 sq. yards = 25.08 sq. metres). Calculate from the verified yard extent and ignore stale disagreements about the derived metre value; unresolved yard extents remain blank. Guardian downloads must identify the specific party and missing field before download, and complete guardian/ward/court particulars must survive in the generated deed.
